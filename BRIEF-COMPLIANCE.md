# Databricks brief: requirement and evidence audit

Reviewed 4 October 2026 against the retained challenge brief and current local artifacts. The challenge requires Omnigent, a complete evidence-driven discovery loop, two candidate tests with a justified choice, a measured bottleneck improvement and responsible source/approval handling. This is an implementation assessment, not an organizer eligibility ruling.

The original brief and diagrams were reviewed before implementation. The judging weights are 30% orchestration, 25% breakthrough potential, 20% acceleration and learning, 15% rigor, and 10% creativity and responsibility. [JURY-REVIEW-MAP.md](reviews/JURY-REVIEW-MAP.md) links evidence for each criterion.

## What the reviewer is seeing

The six-gene panel is a **saved deterministic comparison with curated source context**. The historical UBA3 page is a **replay of a completed Omnigent investigation**. The separate launcher runs actual specialist agents. Browsing does not start a new workflow, and fixed-rule panel rankings are not presented as agent discoveries.

## Next validation priorities

1. Evaluate a predetermined set of cases against a fixed-rule baseline, under matched budgets. Score numerical faithfulness, source attribution, justified uncertainty and useful follow-up reasoning.
2. Recheck source attribution after the context-label correction and resolve the GEO/supplement discrepancy before claiming statistical reproduction.
3. Collect independent biological readouts of senescence, target suppression, cell identity and fitness for a proposed candidate; count robustness alone cannot answer these questions.
4. Refresh the two-minute demo and review the exact repository/video/form artifacts before an authorized publication or submission.

More roles or models should be added only when a specific scientific question requires their outputs. The challenge explicitly allows computational tests and proposed wet-lab follow-up; it does not require demonstrating a 10× improvement within 24 hours.
